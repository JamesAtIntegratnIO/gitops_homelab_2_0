kargo-observability is installed, but kube-state-metrics is not yet reading the
config it wrote. One more step, in the kube-state-metrics values:

  customResourceState:
    enabled: true
    create: false
    name: {{ .Values.kubeStateMetrics.configMapName }}
    key: {{ .Values.kubeStateMetrics.configMapKey }}

`create: false` is the important part -- if kube-state-metrics also creates that
ConfigMap, the two charts fight over it on every sync.

Then confirm the metrics arrived:

  count by (phase) ({{ include "kargo-observability.prefix" . }}_promotion_phase == 1)

If that is empty, the {{ include "kargo-observability.prefix" . }}MetricsMissing
alert will fire in {{ .Values.alerts.rules.metricsMissing.for }} -- which is the
point of it, because a silently-empty exporter looks exactly like a pipeline
with nothing wrong.
